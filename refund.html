Refund Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025  ·  7-day refund guarantee on new paid subscriptions.

1. Overview

At HireFlow, operated by FutureHire Technologies, we are committed to your satisfaction. We offer a 7-day refund guarantee on new paid subscriptions to give you peace of mind when trying our platform. This policy outlines the conditions under which refunds are granted and the process for requesting one.

2. Refund Eligibility

2.1 Eligible for a Refund

  • New subscribers on the Recruiter Plan (₹999/month) or Premium Plan (₹7,999/year) who request a refund within 7 days of their first payment.
  • Situations where a duplicate payment was charged due to a technical error on our platform.
  • Cases where HireFlow permanently discontinues the service and you have a remaining prepaid subscription period.

2.2 Not Eligible for a Refund

  • Refund requests submitted more than 7 days after the initial payment date.
  • Subscription renewal charges — the 7-day guarantee applies only to the first payment of a new subscription, not to renewal payments.
  • Free trial users — no payment is collected during the free trial, so no refund applies.
  • Accounts suspended or terminated due to violation of our Terms and Conditions.
  • Partial-month or partial-year refunds for mid-cycle cancellations after the 7-day window.
  • Requests citing reasons such as "I forgot to cancel before renewal" after the renewal date has passed.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund within the 7-day eligibility window, please follow these steps:

  • Step 1: Send an email to futurehiretech@gmail.com with the subject line: "Refund Request — [Your Registered Email]"
  • Step 2: Include the following information in your email:
    • Your full name and registered email address
    • The subscription plan you purchased
    • The date of payment
    • Your Razorpay transaction ID or payment receipt (if available)
    • A brief reason for the refund request (optional but helpful)
  • Step 3: Our support team will review your request and respond within 2–3 business days.
  • Step 4: If approved, the refund will be processed within 7–10 business days to the original payment method via Razorpay.

4. Refund Processing

  • Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment method (credit card, debit card, UPI, net banking, or digital wallet) used at the time of purchase.
  • Refunds are processed through Razorpay. Once initiated by us, the credit typically appears within 7–10 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.
  • We are not responsible for delays caused by your bank or payment provider after the refund has been initiated on our end.
  • Refunds will be issued in Indian Rupees (INR) for the exact amount charged. We are not responsible for currency conversion losses if you paid in a different currency.

5. Subscription Cancellation vs. Refund

Cancelling your subscription and requesting a refund are two separate actions:

  • Cancellation stops future billing but does not automatically trigger a refund. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.
  • Refund returns the money paid and terminates access to the Platform once the refund is processed.
  • If you request a refund, please also cancel your subscription in your account settings to prevent future charges.

6. Exceptional Circumstances

We understand that exceptional situations may arise. If you have a refund request that falls outside the standard eligibility criteria (e.g., serious health issues, bereavement, or significant platform outages), please contact us at futurehiretech@gmail.com. We will review such requests on a case-by-case basis at our sole discretion.

7. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
