Frequently Asked Questions
What happens after the free trial ends?
Your automation access is paused. You can upgrade to the ₹499/month plan at any time to continue applying. All your saved settings are preserved.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel anytime from your dashboard before the renewal date. No questions asked, no hidden fees.
What payment methods are accepted?
All major credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and wallets via Razorpay — India's most trusted payment gateway.
Can I change my locked Dice email?
For security, your Dice email is locked per subscription. Contact support@diceautoapply.com if you need to transfer to a different account.
