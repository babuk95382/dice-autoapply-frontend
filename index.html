FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What subscription plans does HireFlow offer? ›
HireFlow offers a 3-day Free Trial at no cost, a Growth Plan at ₹499/month for active recruiting teams, and a Enterprise Yearly Plan at ₹7,999/year for established agencies. All paid plans include a 7-day refund guarantee.
Who can use HireFlow? ›
HireFlow is designed for recruiters, HR teams, staffing professionals, talent acquisition specialists, and hiring managers at organizations of all sizes. Job seekers can also access the platform to manage their own application tracking and workflows.
How does ATS resume screening work? ›
HireFlow's ATS resume screening tools allow you to upload, parse, and filter candidate resumes based on job requirements, skills, and qualifications. The system helps you quickly identify the most suitable candidates and organize them by relevance within your recruitment pipeline.
Can I try HireFlow before paying? ›
Yes. HireFlow offers a 3-day free trial with access to core platform features including candidate management and workflow tools. No payment information is required to start your trial. Upgrade to a paid plan at any time to unlock full features.
How many candidates can I manage? ›
The Recruiter Plan and Premium Plan support management of up to 10 active candidates simultaneously, allowing parallel recruitment workflow management. You can manage an unlimited number of candidate records in your database.
What payment methods are accepted? ›
All major credit and debit cards, UPI, net banking, and digital wallets are accepted via trusted and secure payment partners — India's most trusted and secure payment gateway. All transactions are encrypted and protected by 256-bit SSL security.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime? ›
Yes. You can cancel your HireFlow subscription at any time from your account dashboard before the renewal date. No questions asked, no hidden cancellation fees. Your data remains accessible until the end of your current billing period.
How do I get support? ›
Our support team is available via email at Support@futurehiretech.com. Recruiter and Premium plan subscribers receive priority support with faster response times. We also provide onboarding assistance to help you get started quickly.
