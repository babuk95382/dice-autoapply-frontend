Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025  ·  Effective immediately upon account registration

1. Introduction

Welcome to HireFlow ("we", "our", "us", or "the Platform"), a subscription-based recruitment workflow management platform operated by FutureHire Technologies. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform at any HireFlow-operated website or service.

By accessing or using HireFlow, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our services immediately.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Registration Information

  • Full name (first and last)
  • Email address
  • Phone / contact number
  • Password (stored as a secure hash — never in plain text)
  • Account type (Candidate, Recruiter, or HR Professional)

2.2 Recruiter and Candidate Workflow Data

  • Job titles, target roles, and search parameters configured by you
  • Candidate profiles, resumes, and application history
  • Recruitment pipeline stages and workflow configurations
  • Notes, tags, and candidate evaluation data entered by recruiters
  • Run logs, workflow execution history, and automation results

2.3 ATS Resume Screening Data

  • Resume files and documents uploaded for screening
  • Parsed resume content (skills, experience, education)
  • Screening criteria, scores, and rankings generated by the platform
  • Candidate shortlisting decisions and notes

2.4 Payment Information

  • Subscription plan selected and billing period
  • Payment transaction IDs (provided by Razorpay)
  • Billing history and invoice records
  • We do NOT store credit card numbers, CVVs, or banking credentials — all payment processing is handled by Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure

2.5 Technical and Usage Data

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type, version, and operating system
  • Device information and screen resolution
  • Pages visited, features used, and time spent on the platform
  • Session data and authentication tokens
  • Error logs and diagnostic information

2.6 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to maintain your session, remember preferences, and improve platform performance. Types of cookies used:

  • Essential cookies: Required for login sessions and platform functionality
  • Performance cookies: Help us understand how users interact with the platform
  • Preference cookies: Remember your settings such as theme preferences

You can disable cookies through your browser settings, but this may limit platform functionality.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the HireFlow platform and its features
  • To process subscription payments and manage your account billing
  • To facilitate ATS resume screening, candidate management, and recruitment workflows
  • To send transactional emails (account verification, password reset, subscription confirmations)
  • To send subscription renewal reminders and important platform notices
  • To provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve platform features and performance
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted partners who assist with platform operations (Supabase for database hosting, Razorpay for payment processing, SMTP providers for email delivery)
  • Legal Compliance: When required by applicable law, court order, or governmental authority
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or a portion of our assets
  • Consent: With your explicit consent in any other circumstances

All service providers are contractually obligated to handle your data securely and only for the purposes for which it was shared.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Specifically:

  • Account data: Retained for the duration of your account plus 90 days after account deletion
  • Payment records: Retained for 7 years for financial compliance and dispute resolution
  • Run logs and workflow history: Retained for 12 months after your last active subscription
  • ATS screening data: Retained for the duration of your active subscription
  • Support communications: Retained for 2 years

After applicable retention periods, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

6. Account Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS with TLS encryption
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — we cannot view your password
  • Database access is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Session tokens are invalidated upon logout

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials. Notify us immediately at futurehiretech@gmail.com if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable, machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to certain types of data processing
  • Withdrawal: Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, contact us at futurehiretech@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

HireFlow is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you become aware that a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

HireFlow operates primarily in India. If you access the platform from outside India, your data may be transferred to and processed in India or other countries where our service providers operate. By using our platform, you consent to such transfers.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
